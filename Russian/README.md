# Russian


### Alphabet

##### Same pronunciation as english
- A f**a**ther
- K **k**ind
- M **m**en
- O **o**ld
- T **t**ake

##### English characters but different sounds

- В **V**ibes
- Е **Ye**s
- Н **N**ice
- Р **R**ain
- С **S**un
- Y S**oo**n
- Х **H**ello

##### New characters but known sounds
- Б **B**aby
- Г **G**ood
- Д **D**ate
- Ё **Yo**ur
- З **Z**odiac
- И M**ee**t
- Й To**y**
- Л **L**ove
- П **P**eople
- Ф **F**ine
- Э **E**dit
- Ю **U**niversity
- Я **Ya**rd


##### New characters and new sounds
- Ж Plea**s**ure
- Ц I**ts**
- Ч **Ch**ips
- Ш **sh**ape
- щ **sh**eep (softer)
- Ъ (this is a small pause in the word, known as Hard Sign)
- Ы (special sound, mouth, lip and tongue position of the sound r**u**le, but you say the letter I instead like the I in s**i**t) 
- Ь (Indicates that you have to pronounce the preceding consonant softer)
